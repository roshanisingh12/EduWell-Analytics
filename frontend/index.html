How EduWell Analytics works

  1. Upload a CSV with the eight required columns, or use the synthetic sample (2,000 generated students).
  2. Cleaning removes duplicates, incomplete rows, unknown stress labels and out-of-range values. Nothing is imputed.
  3. Statistics cover means, spread, skewness, stress-group comparisons (ANOVA, η²) and Pearson/Spearman correlations.
  4. Insights are generated from the computed numbers and can be downloaded as text, CSV, JSON or a ZIP bundle with figures.

Uploads are processed on the server, kept only for your browser session (they expire after a period of inactivity) and are never shown to other visitors.

The results describe statistical relationships in the data. They are not a diagnosis of any mental-health condition.
